# Week 14 – Geographical Analysis Using Tableau Public

## 1. Objective

To perform geographical analysis using Tableau Public by visualizing location-based data through maps and charts. The objective is to identify regional sales and profit patterns and understand geographical variations in business performance.

## 2. Dataset Used

**Dataset:** Global Superstore Dataset

**Source:** Kaggle

**Description:** The dataset contains global retail sales information, including customer details, product categories, countries, states, regions, sales, profit and shipping details.

## 3. Visualizations Created

Four visualizations were created using Tableau Public:

1. **Sales by Country:** A geographical map showing the distribution of sales across different countries.
2. **Profit by State:** A geographical map displaying profit variations across states.
3. **Sales by Region:** A bar chart comparing total sales across different regions.
4. **Profit by Region:** A bar chart comparing total profit across different regions.

## 4. Interactive Filters

Two interactive filters were added to the dashboard:

1. **Country Filter:** Allows users to view geographical data for selected countries.
2. **Region Filter:** Allows users to analyze sales and profit for specific regions.

Both filters are applied across worksheets using the same data source.

## 5. Key Findings

1. The Central region generates the highest sales among the regions, followed by the South region.
2. The Central region also records the highest overall profit compared with other regions.
3. Sales and profit vary significantly across countries and geographical locations, indicating differences in regional business performance.

## 6. Suggestions and Recommendations

1. The company should focus on expanding business opportunities in regions with lower sales and profit.
2. The company should study successful regions such as Central and use their business strategies to improve performance in other regions.

## 7. Conclusion

The Global Superstore geographical analysis dashboard provides a clear understanding of sales and profit distribution across different countries, states and regions. The maps help identify geographical variations, while the bar charts make regional comparisons easier. Interactive Country and Region filters allow users to explore the data dynamically. Overall, this analysis helps businesses identify strong and weak geographical markets and make better data-driven decisions.
